Tere Ishq Mein Movie Review: The bilingual musical action romance drama film “Tere Ishq Mein” marks Dhanush’s return to Bollywood after a 4-year hiatus and the first ever pairing with the dynamic Kriti Sanon. The movie has Dhanush and Kriti portraying prominent roles in a story that gives the right blend of obsession, transformation, heartbreak, and raw emotional violence. Tere Ishq Mein is directed by Aanand L. Rai, and it marks a return to the explosive, character-driven storytelling he’s known for. In this Tere Ishq Mein movie review, we will try to break the film down across story, direction, performances, music, technicals, and overall impact.

## Tere Ishq Mein Storyline

The story takes us to Shankar Gurukkal (Dhanush), who is the notorious, hot-headed president of DUSU. His violent tendencies make him feared across campus. On the other hand, Mukti Behniwal (Kriti Sanon) is a PhD student whose thesis argues that even the most violent men can be transformed with the right psychological approach.

Her theory creates havoc, and it gets tested when Shankar bursts into her presentation and creates a chaotic scene. The professors instantly dismiss her idea. However, Mukti is confident about the idea, and she challenges them and volunteers to reform Shankar herself. Shankar was reluctant at first, but eventually he allowed her into his world. It started as an academic experiment that soon turned into a powerful, emotionally messy romance. As time passes, Shankar softens, Mukti completes her thesis, and the relationship appears picture-perfect until reality strikes.

The story then jumps 7 years ahead, and eventually fate brings them face-to-face again. This takes us to a darker, more complicated chapter that is filled with obsession, regret, and unresolved trauma.

## Direction and Screenplay: Strong Drama, Weak Second Half

Himanshu Sharma and Neeraj Yadav have done magic as they crafted a screenplay that starts grippingly and eventually builds excellent emotional tension. The first half takes us to:

* Sharp drama

* Engaging character dynamics

* Humor sprinkled through intense situations

* Strong narrative momentum

Aanand L. Rai uses a smart structure. The movie starts at a key turning point, then rewinds the timeline. Several scenes stand out: Mukti challenging Shankar, the bar chaos, the intermission twist, and the sequences involving Shankar’s father, Raghav (Prakash Raj).

However, the second half stumbles. The present-day storyline looks dragged, and it becomes confusing, which ultimately reduces impact.

## Performances: Dhanush & Kriti Sanon Are Outstanding

The film rests heavily on its performances, and both the talented leads didn’t disappoint.

**Dhanush as Shankar**

A powerhouse performance. He captures rage. Dhanush has shown his vulnerability, heartbreak, and madness with raw precision. His emotional breakdown is hard-hitting, thanks to the scenes and transitional arcs.

**Kriti Sanon as Mukti**

This is one of Kriti’s most layered portrayals. She has shown her class as she presents the right balance of intelligence, compassion, inner conflict, and emotional restraint. She stands tall in a narrative that could have easily been overshadowed by Dhanush.

**Supporting Cast**

* Prakash Raj shines as Raghav and has delivered some of the most memorable emotional beats.

* Priyanshu Painyuli supports Shankar’s best friend, and it feels so natural.

* Viineet Kumar Singh was excellent, as he impressed in a short but powerful appearance.

Characters that include Tota Roy Chowdhury, Chittaranjan Tripathy, and Mohd Zeeshan Ayyub add value where needed.

## Music, VFX & Technicals: A.R. Rahman Elevates the Emotion

The music album is rich and deeply atmospheric, all thanks to the classic A.R. Rahman.

* ‘Jigar Thanda’ is the standout song from the movie.

* ‘Chinnaware’ is a full Tamil track in a Hindi film that adds uniqueness and comes with that flair that we all need.

* The background score is very strong, as it intensifies the emotional shifts.

## Final Verdict: A Passionate Saga with Flaws but Strong Heart

Overall, Tere Ishq Mein is an intense love saga, which is powered by phenomenal performances and strong musical depth. The overlong runtime and weaker second half drag its impact. But Dhanush and Kriti’s chemistry and emotional firepower make it a compelling one-time watch.
